ABOUT THE PROJECT

Volunteer Management System

Our objective

The objective of this system is to connect volunteers with organizations that need help for different community activities and projects.

For Volunteers

Volunteers can register, create and view their profile, search for opportunities, apply for them and monitor their applications.

For Admin

Organizations can create volunteer opportunities, manage them and review applications submitted by volunteers ,assign to activities and track volunteer participation.
